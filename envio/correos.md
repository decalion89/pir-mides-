## 1. ScanPyramids (HIP Institute y Facultad de Ingeniería, Universidad de El Cairo)

**Asunto:** Independent reanalysis of the 2017 Big Void figures and a proposed King's Chamber measurement

Dear ScanPyramids team,

I am an independent researcher. I have reanalysed the data published in your 2017 *Nature* paper on the Big Void, digitizing Extended Data Figs. 2 and 4 and fitting them with an open forward model. Using only your simulated Grand Gallery, the model recovers your emulsion positions to within 0.2 m. The preprint is here: [arXiv link].

Main points:
- The published figures cannot fix the void's inclination, and the KEK and emulsion data sets appear mutually inconsistent under simple shapes (Δχ² ≈ 78). We think a systematic is responsible, which only the original maps can resolve.
- A Cramér–Rao calculation and blind tests suggest that a **stereo analysis of the full NE1+NE2 maps** could already give a ~3σ preference between shapes, and that a ~1 m² detector in the **King's Chamber** for ~2 months would be decisive.
- All predictions are registered in advance, and the preprint documents four errors we found and corrected in our own work.

The code and data are open: https://github.com/decalion89/pir-mides-. I would be grateful for any feedback, and happy to collaborate if useful.

## 2. Andrzej Ćwiek (Universidad Adam Mickiewicz, Poznań): experimento del trineo

**Asunto:** Sledge haulage at Biskupin: did you measure corner-turning times?

Dear Dr Ćwiek,

I read about your sledge-hauling experiments at Biskupin (2001, 2019) and your observation on levering the load to start it moving or to turn it. In a construction model of the Great Pyramid, I found that the **time a loaded sledge occupies a 90° corner** is the single parameter that decides whether one ramp could have sufficed. Our pre-registered thresholds are ≤ 2.1 min per corner for stop-and-pivot, or a rope-on-post friction μ ≤ 0.4.

Do your experiments include turning times or rope-redirect measurements? If not, would you be interested in a short joint test? A one-page protocol is attached (PROPUESTA_EXPERIMENTO_TRINEO.md).
